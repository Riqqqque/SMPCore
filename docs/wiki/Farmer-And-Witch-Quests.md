# Farmer and Witch Quests

## Rowan the Farmer

Rowan's twelve medium-difficulty commissions are split into three chapters.

### Chapter I: Fieldwork

Gather mixed seeds and crops, harvest mature rows, work vines and orchards, and fill the granary. Completing the fourth commission awards **Furrowkeeper**, a Netherite Hoe with Efficiency V, Fortune III, Unbreaking III, Mending, Replenish I, Harvesting III, and Wise II.

### Chapter II: The Kitchen

Turn in bread, baked food, soups, stews, cooked proteins, pies, cakes, and a final banquet. Completing the eighth commission unlocks **Tiller, the Sprout Mole**.

While summoned, Tiller grants:

- A 10% chance for one extra crop from a mature harvest.
- A 10% chance for one extra serving from a normal food craft.

### Chapter III: Harvest Mastery

Craft food and complete a large harvest with Tiller active, stock a full larder, and prepare the First Feast. Finishing the chapter awakens **Hearty Harvest**: food crafted with Tiller active has a 5% chance to receive double its normal hunger and saturation values. The exact upgraded values are written on the food's lore.

## Vespera the Hedge-Witch

Vespera has eight lessons covering overworld reagents, Nether ingredients, ordinary potion families, thrown potion practice, lingering brews, rare catalysts, and controlled brewing. Her Moonlit Thesis requires advanced vanilla potions and Dragon's Breath.

Completing the final lesson unlocks **Morrow, the Rosy Moonmoth** and permanent access to boss-material potion recipes.

While summoned, Morrow grants:

- Every newly brewed vanilla effect potion receives a random 15-30% duration increase.
- Each thrown splash or lingering potion has a 5% chance to gain one valid vanilla level.
- Already-maxed effects and boss potions are never upgraded.

Boss materials cannot be inserted into a brewing stand until the Moonlit Thesis is complete. Hoppers and other automation cannot bypass the unlock.

## Familiar Rules

Only one familiar can be active at a time. Use `/menu` and open **Familiars** to manage the Veil Wisp, Miner Familiar, Tiller, or Morrow.
