# Superpowers

Players receive one hidden superpower on first join. The power is meant to be discovered through gameplay. If the power has a command, the player privately receives a hint.

Use `/powerinfo` to read about classes. Staff can assign powers with `/setpower <player> <power>`. Ancient Scroll rerolls a player's power, even if they already have one.

The `/powerinfo` menu is sorted from most common to rarest.

## Power Chances

- `Juggernaut` - 7.41%. Tank identity with extra health and crouch knockback resistance.
- `Verdant` - 7.41%. Crop, wood, healing, and Stick from Mother Nature gameplay.
- `Titan` - 6.17%. Larger body, extra hearts, heavy melee damage, and lower knockback.
- `Stormrunner` - 5.76%. Permanent speed and haste.
- `Warborn` - 5.76%. Strength fighter with low-health speed and regeneration.
- `Prospector` - 5.35%. Mining power with haste, extra ore chances, and extra health.
- `Nightshade` - 4.53%. `/shadow toggle`, invisible gear hiding, and speed while hidden.
- `Deadeye` - 4.12%. Ranged mark pressure and anti-stealth utility.
- `Frostborn` - 4.12%. Chill-based combat pressure and defensive retaliation.
- `Sentinel` - 4.12%. Defensive guard identity.
- `Druid` - 3.70%. Right-click Druid's Grimoire to buff self and nearby teammates for 5 minutes.
- `Arcanist` - 3.29%. XP, enchanting, luck, durability, and essence extraction.
- `Oathbound` - 3.29%. Team aura identity for grouped fights.
- `Runesmith` - 3.29%. Durability protection and boss-kill repair flavor.
- `Sovereign` - 3.29%. `/msummon <amount>` stores killed mobs and summons them later.
- `The World` - 3.29%. Bound clock that stops time in a radius.
- `Graveborn` - 2.88%. Undead resistance and healing from kills.
- `Riftwarden` - 2.88%. Boss-hunter with Slow Falling near custom bosses, plus Resistance near bosses or in the End.
- `Stormcaller` - 2.88%. Storm and lightning combat identity. Use `/stormcaller off` if you want to disable strike procs.
- `Voidwalker` - 2.88%. `/voidstep` and `/voidvision`, with toggleable night vision refreshed before blinking.
- `Bloodmender` - 2.47%. Sustain fighter that heals from damage dealt.
- `Oracle Eye` - 2.47%. `/xray` highlights players, entities, and ores for a short window.
- `Skybound` - 2.06%. Creative-style flight burst with a cooldown.
- `Wayfarer` - 2.06%. `/travel <x> <y> <z> <dimension>` and `/travel close` for portal control.
- `Ashen Soul` - 1.65%. Fire-themed survival and death-prevention flavor.
- `Tideborn` - 1.65%. Strong water buffs, underwater breathing, and normal underwater mining.
- `Mortal` - 1.23%. No special power until rerolled with Ancient Scroll.
- `Deathless` - 0.01%. Lethal damage leaves the player barely alive with extreme recovery.

## Bound Power Items

- `World Clock` belongs to The World.
- `Stick from Mother Nature` belongs to Verdant.
- `Druid's Grimoire` belongs to Druid.

Bound power items are preserved through death and stay tied to their owner.
