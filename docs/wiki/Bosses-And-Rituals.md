# Bosses and Rituals

Bosses can be spawned by staff through `/bosses`, but survival players use rituals from `/bossrituals`.

Every boss has:

- A boss bar.
- A live hologram.
- Phase mechanics.
- Custom particles and sounds.
- Tracked cleanup so staff can remove stale bosses.
- Boss trophy drops for Covenant recipes.
- Public kill announcements.
- Server-wide damage leaderboards after each fight.
- Personal after-action reports in `/leaderboards`.
- Double boss drops from 4-6 PM mountain time by default.
- Player-count rage scaling.
- Anti-cheese arena pressure on risky edge camping or high-ground camping.
- Bosses despawn as a failed fight if the arena wipes after the fight starts.

## Existing Bosses

### Yule the Minion

- Fast normal-sized zombie in gold armor.
- Phase 2 gains Strength and heavier knockback.
- Summons Yule's Thralls in phase 2.
- Drops Gilded Skull and rare Oathbound Plate.

### Kael the Ashen

- Skeleton marksman.
- Phase 2 fires harsher burning control shots.
- Drops Solar Ember and rare Titan Gear.

### Vesper the Widow Queen

- Spider boss focused on leap pressure, poison, and dragging strikes.
- Phase 2 is faster and punishes spacing harder.
- Drops Widow Silk and rare Verdant Heart.

### Voralith the Crimson Warden

- Tough Warden boss with dominion pulses and crimson resonance.
- Drops Dominion Core, Crimson Rib, and Sculk Heart.
- Dominion Core repairs Crimson Dominion in an anvil.

## New Bosses

- Enderman boss themed around portals and displacement.
- Phase 2 increases speed and rift pressure.
- Drops Rift Lens, rare Void Halo, and has a 50% chance to drop an Awakening Table.

### Nereida the Abyss Mother

- Drowned boss that thrives in water or rain.
- Phase 2 gains Resistance and stronger wave pressure.
- Drops Abyssal Pearl and rare Tideheart.

### The Iron Saint

- Iron Golem boss built around slow, heavy punishment.
- Phase 2 adds stronger slam pulses and Weakness pressure.
- Drops Titan Gear and rare Saint Alloy.

### Mirewood the Root Tyrant

- Husk boss wrapped in living roots.
- Phase 2 regenerates and spreads stronger root snares.
- Drops Living Bark and rare Verdant Heart.

## Ritual Tips

- Open `/bossrituals` for exact shrine descriptions.
- Right-click the focus block or any nearby shrine block with the listed catalyst.
- Successful rituals consume the shrine blocks.
- Failed rituals tell you what part of the pattern is missing.
- Bigger bosses may create arena boundaries.
- If more players join a fight, the boss can gain extra health and damage scaling.
- Arena hazards are meant to stop cheesing, not punish normal fighting.

## Boss Brews

Boss materials can be brewed into stronger potions.

- Open `/bossbrews`, `/bosspotions`, or `/brews`.
- Use an Awkward Potion, Splash Awkward Potion, or Lingering Awkward Potion.
- Put one listed boss material in the ingredient slot.
- The output keeps the same potion form and gains the custom boss-brew effects.

Current brews include Sunforged Ichor, Dominion Blood, Rift Draught, Abyssal Tonic, Verdant Elixir, Saint's Resolve, and Widowstep Vial.
