# Covenant Armory

The Covenant Armory is the boss-forged item path inside `/reliquary`.

Covenant gear is meant to sit just above standard legendary strength because it costs public boss trophies and progression materials. Mythic Covenant relics announce to the server when they are forged.

It adds:

- 13 custom weapons and tools.
- 5 full armor sets, each with a four-piece set bonus.
- 11 standalone armor pieces with individual passives.
- 15 utility relics.
- Boss trophies used as crafting materials.

## Weapons

- `Ashen Verdict` - bonus damage and short fire pressure.
- `Widowfang` - cooldown poison and slowness.
- `Rift Pike` - periodically pulls targets toward the wielder.
- `Saintsplitter` - bonus damage and cooldown Weakness.
- `Tidebreaker` - deals extra damage in water or rain.
- `Gravemourn` - small lifesteal on a short cooldown.
- `Nullglass Rapier` - removes one beneficial effect on cooldown.
- `Sunless Repeater` - crossbow bolts inflict Wither and Darkness.
- `Thornwhisper` - arrows apply strong Slowness.
- `Cindershard Dagger` - backstabs deal extra damage and give the wielder speed.
- `Oathbreaker Mattock` - boss-focused mining weapon. Hurts custom bosses harder and can weaken targets.
- `Duskbell Mallet` - mace with a short sonic burst around the impact target.
- `Briarhook Saw` - axe that slows enemies and punishes wounded targets.

## Crafting

Open `/reliquary`, choose `Covenant Armory`, choose a category, then click any item to view and craft it. Crafting consumes the shown materials from normal storage slots and does not consume equipped armor or offhand items.

The intended progression is to kill early bosses for trophy materials, craft safer utility and armor, then use those upgrades to challenge harder rituals. Boss-crafted gear should feel stronger than normal loot, but most effects use cooldowns, ally checks, or short windows so PvP stays playable.
