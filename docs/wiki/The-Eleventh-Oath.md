# The Eleventh Oath

Something beneath Veilward has started ringing.

The ten bosses below the settlement are not unrelated monsters. Each carries a damaged memory from the Dominion that built the Veil. Defeating one with your party recovers its witness statement for every eligible participant and adds it to your personal journal.

Use `/veil` to open the journal. It tracks your current objective, the people you have met, recovered boss memories, the meaning behind systems such as Soul Imprints and Awakening, your Veilmark, and unresolved leads. Locked truths stay marked as `???` until you actually find them.

Useful commands:

- `/veil` or `/veil journal` - open the journal.
- `/veil objective` - show the next narrative lead.
- `/veil memories` - list recovered boss statements in progression order.
- `/veil text` - show a simple chat version, useful on Bedrock.
- `/veil skip` - skip a personal dialogue sequence.

The story follows the systems already in the season. Mayor Bah's orders, Malakar's dungeon, Orin's gear lessons, Soul Imprints, Awakening, Corruption, familiars, and the side quests all reveal different parts of the same history. No story decision changes combat power or boss loot.
